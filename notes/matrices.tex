=====================================
6. MATRICES AND DETERMINANTS
=====================================

2x2 Determinant:
$$\det \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} = ad - bc$$

3x3 Determinant (cofactor expansion along row 1):
$$\det \begin{vmatrix} a & b & c \\ d & e & f \\ g & h & i \end{vmatrix} = a(ei - fh) - b(di - fg) + c(dh - eg)$$

2x2 Inverse (exists only when det A \ne 0):
$$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A} \begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix}$$

Identity matrices:
$$I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad I_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$
$$A \cdot I = I \cdot A = A$$

Matrix equation AX = B:
$$\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} e \\ f \end{bmatrix} \qquad X = A^{-1} B$$

Worked example (from ICTM 2024 15):
$$\begin{bmatrix} 7 & 6 \\ 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} k \\ w \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 30 \\ 15 \end{bmatrix}$$

Singular matrix (no unique solution exists):
$$\det A = 0 \quad \to \quad A \;\text{is singular}$$

Augmented matrix for 2x2 system (ax + by = e, cx + dy = f):
$$\left[ \begin{array}{cc|c} a & b & e \\ c & d & f \end{array} \right]$$

Augmented matrix for 3x3 system:
$$\left[ \begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -4 & -1 & b_1 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 & 2b_1 + b_3 \\ 0 & 4 & 2 & 4b_1 + b_2 \end{array} \right]$$

Matrix product entry formula (A is m x n, B is n x p):
$$(AB)_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^n A_{ik} \cdot B_{kj}$$

Labelled matrix product (using underbrace):
$$A = \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}}_{\text{swap}} \times \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -k & 1 \end{bmatrix}}_{\text{add}}$$

Cramer's Rule (2x2 System):
$$x = \frac{\det \begin{bmatrix} e & b \\ f & d \end{bmatrix}}{\det \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}} \qquad y = \frac{\det \begin{bmatrix} a & e \\ c & f \end{bmatrix}}{\det \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}}$$
