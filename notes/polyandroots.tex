=====================================
POLYNOMIALS AND ROOTS
=====================================

26. Remainder theorem
When f(x) is divided by (x - c), the remainder equals f(c).

27. Factor theorem
(x - c) is a factor of f(x)  iff  f(c) = 0.

28. Quadratic formula
$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} $$

29. Discriminant  (Delta = b^2 - 4ac)
$$ \Delta > 0 \quad \text{two distinct real roots} $$
$$ \Delta = 0 \quad \text{one double root} $$
$$ \Delta < 0 \quad \text{two complex conjugate roots} $$

30. Tangent to x-axis condition
$$ y = ax^2 + bx + c \;\text{ tangent to x-axis iff }\; b^2 = 4ac $$

31. Vieta's formulas -- quadratic  (roots r_1 and r_2)
$$ r_1 + r_2 = -\frac{b}{a} \qquad r_1 \cdot r_2 = \frac{c}{a} $$

32. Sum of squares of roots  (derived from Vieta's)
$$ r_1^2 + r_2^2 = (r_1 + r_2)^2 - 2\,r_1 r_2 $$

33. Absolute difference of roots
$$ \left| r_1 - r_2 \right| = \frac{\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{\left| a \right|} $$

34. Polynomial with given zeros z_1, z_2, ..., z_n  (leading coefficient 1)
$$ f(x) = (x - z_1)(x - z_2) \cdots (x - z_n) $$

35. Finding a shared root of two polynomials
Subtract the two equations to cancel the highest-degree shared terms,
solve for the shared root, then substitute back.
