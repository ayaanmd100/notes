=====================================
4. POLYNOMIALS, ROOTS, AND QUADRATICS
=====================================

Remainder and Factor Theorems:
When f(x) is divided by (x - c), the remainder equals f(c).
(x - c) is a factor of f(x) iff f(c) = 0.

Rational Root Theorem:
For any rational root p/q, p is a factor of the constant term and q is a factor of the leading coefficient.

Quadratic Formula and Discriminant:
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$
$$\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$$
If \Delta > 0, two distinct real roots.
If \Delta = 0, one double root.
If \Delta < 0, two complex conjugate roots.

Tangent to x-axis condition:
$$y = ax^2 + bx + c \quad \text{tangent to x-axis iff} \quad b^2 = 4ac$$

Vieta's Formulas (Quadratic):
$$r_1 + r_2 = \frac{-b}{a}$$
$$r_1 \cdot r_2 = \frac{c}{a}$$

Sum of Squares of Roots:
$$r_1^2 + r_2^2 = (r_1 + r_2)^2 - 2 r_1 r_2$$

Absolute Difference of Roots:
$$\left| r_1 - r_2 \right| = \frac{\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{\left| a \right|}$$

Vieta's Formulas (Cubic):
$$r_1+r_2+r_3 = \frac{-b}{a}$$
$$r_1 r_2 + r_1 r_3 + r_2 r_3 = \frac{c}{a}$$
$$r_1 r_2 r_3 = \frac{-d}{a}$$

Polynomial with given zeros z_1, z_2, ..., z_n (leading coefficient 1):
$$f(x) = (x - z_1)(x - z_2) \cdot \cdot \cdot (x - z_n)$$

Finding a shared root of two polynomials:
Subtract the two equations to cancel the highest-degree shared terms, solve for the shared root, then substitute back.
