1. Quadratic: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$

$$ r_1 + r_2 = -\frac{b}{a}, \quad r_1 r_2 = \frac{c}{a} $$

2. Cubic: $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d = 0$

$$ r_1+r_2+r_3 = -\frac{b}{a} $$

$$ r_1r_2 + r_1r_3 + r_2r_3 = \frac{c}{a} $$

$$ r_1r_2r_3 = -\frac{d}{a} $$

3. Sum of squares of roots

$$ r_1^2+r_2^2+r_3^2 = (r_1+r_2+r_3)^2 - 2(r_1r_2+r_1r_3+r_2r_3) $$

4. Building polynomial from roots $r_1, r_2, r_3$:

$$ x^3 - (r_1+r_2+r_3)x^2 + (r_1r_2+r_1r_3+r_2r_3)x - r_1r_2r_3 = 0 $$

5. Reciprocal roots: reverse the coefficient list.

6. Common root — subtract equations. Example: $x^3+kx^2-3x+4=0$ and $x^3+kx^2-5x+8=0$.

Subtract: $2x-4=0 \rightarrow x=2$

$$ 8+4k-6+4=0 \rightarrow k=-\frac{3}{2} $$

7. Remainder Theorem: remainder of $f(x)/(x-c)$ equals $f(c)$.

8. Factor Theorem: $(x-c)$ is a factor iff $f(c) = 0$.

9. Two factors known: substitute both roots, solve 2x2 system.

Example: $kx^3+wx^2-5x+84$, factors $(x-7)$ and $(x-4)$

$$ f(7) = 0 \rightarrow 343k+49w = -49 \rightarrow 7k+w=-1 $$

$$ f(4) = 0 \rightarrow 64k+16w=-64 \rightarrow 4k+w=-4 $$

$$ 3k=3 \rightarrow k=1, \quad w=-8 $$
