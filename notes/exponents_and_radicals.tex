1. Exponent rules

$$ a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}, \quad \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}, \quad (a^m)^n = a^{mn} $$

$$ a^0=1, \quad a^{-n}=\frac{1}{a^n}, \quad a^{m/n}=\sqrt[n]{a^m} $$

2. Radical rules

$$ \sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{a}\sqrt{b}, \quad \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} $$

3. Rationalizing

$$ \frac{a}{\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c}} = \frac{a(\sqrt{b}-\sqrt{c})}{b-c} $$

4. Solving radical equations: isolate radical, square, check extraneous.

Example: $-2 = -x + \sqrt{-5x + 46}$

$$ \sqrt{-5x+46} = x-2 \rightarrow x^2+x-42=0 \rightarrow x=6 \; (valid), \; x=-7 \; (extraneous) $$

5. Mixed radical simplification

$$ \sqrt[3]{9} \cdot \sqrt[4]{16} \cdot \sqrt[6]{36} = 3^{2/3} \cdot 2 \cdot 6^{1/3} = 2^{4/3} \cdot 3 = 3\sqrt[3]{4} $$

k=3, w=4, sum=7.
