1. Shoelace formula for area of polygon (vertices listed in order, indices mod n)

$$ A = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i) \right| $$

2. AM-GM: maximize product given fixed linear constraint. Equality when both terms are equal.

Example: $4x + 5y = 20$, maximize $xy$. Set $4x = 5y$ and $4x+5y=20$:

$$ x=2.5, \; y=2 \rightarrow xy_{\max}=5 $$

3. Diophantine: $ax + by = c$ integer solutions. Find one solution, then parameterize.

For $5x + 16y = 38$: need $38 - 16y$ divisible by 5, so $y \equiv 3$ (mod 5).

y=3 gives x=2; y=-2 gives x=14; y=8 gives x=-10.
