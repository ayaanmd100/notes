Nonlinear system (2014 \#9):

Area of polygonal region from inequality system:
1. Find all boundary lines.
2. Solve pairs to get vertices.
3. Apply Shoelace Formula:
A = \frac{1}{2}\left|\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)\right| \quad (indices   mod    n)

Example (2018 \#20):  x+y \le 5,\; 2x-y \ge -4,\; x \ge 0,\; y \ge 0
Find vertices, apply Shoelace.
