1. Permutations (order matters)

$$ P(n,r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} $$

2. Combinations (order does not matter)

$$ \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}, \quad \binom{n}{r} = \binom{n}{n-r} $$

3. 26 letters taken 4 at a time (ordered)

$$ P(26,4) = 26 \cdot 25 \cdot 24 \cdot 23 = 358800 $$

4. $\binom{n}{3}$ formula

$$ \binom{n}{3} = \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{6} $$

5. Passcode: 7 characters, starts with letter (3 choices), ends with number (3 choices), middle 5 are permuted

$$ 3 \times 5! \times 3 = 3 \times 120 \times 3 = 1080 $$

6. Distributing 12 distinct balls equally into 3 containers

$$ \binom{12}{4} \cdot \binom{8}{4} \cdot \binom{4}{4} = 495 \cdot 70 \cdot 1 = 34650 $$

7. k/w ratio example: $k = P(5,5) = 120$, $w = \binom{5}{3} = 10$

$$ \frac{k}{w} = 12 $$
