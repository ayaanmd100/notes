Factorial:  n! = n \cdot (n-1) \cdot (n-2) \cdot\cdot\cdot 2 \cdot 1, \quad 0! = 1

Permutations (order matters):  P(n,r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}

Combinations (order does not matter):  C(n,r) = \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}

\binom{n}{r} = \binom{n}{n-r}

Permutations of all 26 letters taken 4 at a time: 
P(26,4) = 26 \cdot 25 \cdot 24 \cdot 23 = 358800

Combinations example:  \binom{5}{3} = 10, \binom{5}{0}=1, \binom{5}{5}=1

k = P(5,5) = 120, \quad w = C(5,3) = 10, \quad \frac{k}{w} = 12

C(n,3) problems:  \binom{n}{3} = \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{6}

Distributing 12 distinct balls equally into 3 containers (4 each):
\binom{12}{4} \cdot \binom{8}{4} \cdot \binom{4}{4} = 495 \cdot 70 \cdot 1 = 34650
