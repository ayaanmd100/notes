1. Expansion

$$ (a+b)^n = \sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{n}{r} a^{n-r} b^r $$

2. General term

$$ T_{r+1} = \binom{n}{r} a^{n-r} b^r $$

3. Number of terms in $(a+b)^n$: $n + 1$

4. Sum of all coefficients (set $a = b = 1$): $\sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{n}{r} = 2^n$

For $(x + 1/x)^5$: set $x = 1$, sum = $2^5 = 32$.

5. Specific term in $(a+b)^{22}$ containing $b^{20}$: $r=20$

$$ T_{21} = \binom{22}{20} a^2 b^{20} = 231\, a^2 b^{20} $$

k = 231, w = 2, k + w = 233.

6. Equal consecutive terms: $T_{r+1} = T_{r+2}$

$$ \binom{n}{r} = \binom{n}{r+1} \rightarrow r = \frac{n-1}{2} $$

7. Example: 5th and 6th terms of $(x+y)^n$ equal

$$ \binom{n}{4} = \binom{n}{5} \rightarrow n-4=5 \rightarrow n=9 $$

Third term: $T_3 = \binom{9}{2} x^7 y^2 = 36\, x^7 y^2$, so coefficient = 36.

8. Equal numerical coefficients in $(2x+c)^8$, terms $T_4$ and $T_5$

$$ \binom{8}{3} \cdot 2^5 = \binom{8}{4} \cdot 2^4 \cdot c \rightarrow 56 \cdot 32 = 70 \cdot 16 \cdot c \rightarrow c = \frac{8}{5} $$

9. Pascal's identity

$$ \binom{n}{r} + \binom{n}{r+1} = \binom{n+1}{r+1} $$
