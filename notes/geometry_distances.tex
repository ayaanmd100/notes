=====================================
DISTANCE, GEOMETRY AND RADICALS
=====================================
 
71. Distance between two points
$$ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} $$
 
72. Midpoint formula
$$ M = \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \;\; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right) $$
 
73. Distance from point (x_0, y_0) to line  ax + by + c = 0
$$ d = \frac{\left| ax_0 + by_0 + c \right|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}} $$
 
74. Distance between parallel lines  ax + by + c_1 = 0  and  ax + by + c_2 = 0
$$ d = \frac{\left| c_1 - c_2 \right|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}} $$
 
75. Space diagonal of rectangular box  (dimensions l, w, h)
$$ d = \sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2} $$
 
76. Radical product and quotient
$$ \sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab} \qquad \frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} \qquad \sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b} = \sqrt[n]{ab} $$
 
77. Rationalising the denominator
$$ \frac{k}{\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}} = \frac{k\left(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}\right)}{a - b} $$
 
78. Optimising product given fixed sum  (AM-GM applied)
When x + y = c (constant), xy is maximised when x = y = c/2:
$$ (xy)_{\max} = \frac{c^2}{4} $$
