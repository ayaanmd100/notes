1. Quadratic formula

$$ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a} $$

2. Discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$:
- $\Delta > 0$: two distinct real roots
- $\Delta = 0$: one repeated root (tangent to x-axis)
- $\Delta < 0$: two complex conjugate roots

3. Sum and product of roots

$$ r_1 + r_2 = -\frac{b}{a}, \quad r_1 r_2 = \frac{c}{a} $$

4. Difference of roots

$$ |r_1 - r_2| = \frac{\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{|a|} $$

5. Tangent to x-axis: set $\Delta = 0$

$$ y = x^2+16x+2k \rightarrow 256-8k=0 \rightarrow k=32 $$

6. One root is the square of the other

$$ r \cdot r^2 = \frac{c}{a} \rightarrow r^3 = 8 \rightarrow r = 2 \rightarrow k = r+r^2 = 6 $$

7. $f(x) = x$ problems — rearrange to quadratic

$$ 27x^2+32x-10 = x \rightarrow 27x^2+31x-10=0 $$
