Quadratic formula:  x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}

Discriminant:  \Delta = b^2 - 4ac

\begin{array}{ll}
\Delta > 0 & two distinct real roots \\
\Delta = 0 & one repeated (double) root; parabola tangent to  x-axis \\
\Delta < 0 & two complex conjugate roots
\end{array}

Sum of roots:  r_1 + r_2 = -\frac{b}{a}

Product of roots:  r_1 \cdot r_2 = \frac{c}{a}

Difference of roots:  |r_1 - r_2| = \frac{\sqrt{\Delta}}{|a|}

Example (2018 \#17):  2x^2 - kx + 306 = 0,  k > 0, difference of roots = 1
\frac{\sqrt{k^2 - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 306}}{2} = 1
\sqrt{k^2 - 2448} = 2
k^2 - 2448 = 4 \rightarrow k^2 = 2452 \rightarrow k = \sqrt{2452}

Tangent to  x-axis \rightarrow \Delta = 0:
y = x^2 + 16x + 2k \rightarrow 256 - 8k = 0 \rightarrow k = 32

Square root of one root equals the other (2023 \#10):  x^2 - kx + 8 = 0
Let roots be  r  and  r^2:  r \cdot r^2 = 8 \rightarrow r = 2
k = r + r^2 = 2 + 4 = 6
