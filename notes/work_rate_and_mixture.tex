1. Rate * Time = Work. For identical workers: $R_{total} = n \cdot R_{one}$.

2. Man-hours: $M_1 T_1 / W_1 = M_2 T_2 / W_2$

3. Example: 5 mules eat 3 bales in 6 hours. Time for 4 bales with same 5 mules?

$$ R_{per \; mule} = \frac{3}{30} = \frac{1}{10}, \quad t = \frac{4}{5/10} = 8 \; hours $$

4. Mixture: 1 L of 10% + 4 L of k% = 5 L of 8%

$$ 0.1 + 0.04k = 0.4 \rightarrow k = 7.5 $$

5. Dilution: drain x L from 7000 L at 20%, replace with 80%, target 25%

$$ 1400+0.6x = 1750 \rightarrow x = \frac{1750}{3} $$
