=====================================
SEQUENCES AND SERIES
=====================================

15. Arithmetic -- nth term
$$ a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d \qquad d = a_2 - a_1 $$

16. Arithmetic -- sum of n terms
$$ S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n) = \frac{n}{2}\left(2a_1 + (n-1)d\right) $$

17. Geometric -- nth term
$$ a_n = a_1 \cdot r^{n-1} \qquad r = \frac{a_2}{a_1} $$

18. Geometric -- sum of n terms  (r not equal to 1)
$$ S_n = \frac{a_1(1 - r^n)}{1 - r} $$

19. Infinite geometric series  (|r| < 1)
$$ S = \frac{a_1}{1 - r} $$

20. Arithmetic mean
$$ \text{AM} = \frac{a + b}{2} $$

21. Geometric mean
$$ \text{GM} = \sqrt{ab} \qquad a,\, b > 0 $$

22. Harmonic mean
$$ \text{HM} = \frac{2ab}{a + b} $$

23. AM-GM inequality
$$ \frac{a + b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab} \qquad a,\, b \ge 0 $$
Equality holds when a = b.

24. Sum of even integers  2 + 4 + ... + 2n
$$ S = n(n + 1) $$

25. Pell numbers  (P_0 = 0, P_1 = 1)
$$ P_n = 2P_{n-1} + P_{n-2} \qquad n \ge 2 $$
First terms: 0, 1, 2, 5, 12, 29, 70
