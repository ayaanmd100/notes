=====================================
3. SEQUENCES, SERIES, AND MEANS
=====================================

Arithmetic Sequence (nth term):
$$a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d \qquad d = a_2 - a_1$$

Arithmetic Series (Sum of n terms):
$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n) = \frac{n}{2}\left(2a_1 + (n-1)d\right)$$

Geometric Sequence (nth term):
$$a_n = a_1 \cdot r^{n-1} \qquad r = \frac{a_2}{a_1}$$

Geometric Series (Sum of n terms, r \ne 1):
$$S_n = \frac{a_1(1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$$

Infinite Geometric Series (\left| r \right| < 1):
$$S = \frac{a_1}{1 - r}$$

Means (Arithmetic, Geometric, Harmonic):
$$\text{AM} = \frac{a + b}{2}$$
$$\text{GM} = \sqrt{ab} \qquad a, b > 0$$
$$\text{HM} = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$$

AM-GM Inequality (Equality holds when a = b):
$$\frac{a + b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab} \qquad a, b \ge 0$$

Sum of even integers (2 + 4 + \cdot \cdot \cdot + 2n):
$$S = n(n + 1)$$

Sum of first n integers:
$$\sum_{i=1}^n i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

Pell numbers (First terms: 0, 1, 2, 5, 12, 29, 70):
$$P_0 = 0 \qquad P_1 = 1$$
$$P_n = 2P_{n-1} + P_{n-2} \qquad n \ge 2$$
