Definition:  \log_b(x) = n \rightarrow  b^n = x

Common log:  \log(x) = \log_{10}(x)
Natural log:  \ln(x) = \log_e(x)

Product rule:  \log_b(xy) = \log_b x + \log_b y

Quotient rule:  \log_b\!\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_b x - \log_b y

Power rule:  \log_b(x^n) = n \log_b x

Change of base:  \log_b a = \frac{\log a}{\log b} = \frac{\ln a}{\ln b}

\log_b(1) = 0, \quad \log_b(b) = 1, \quad b^{\log_b(x)} = x

\log(1) = 0, \quad \log(10) = 1, \quad \ln(e) = 1

\ln(\exp(x)) = x, \quad \exp(\ln(x)) = x

Key values (memorize):
\log 2 \approx 0.30103, \quad \log 3 \approx 0.47712, \quad \log 7 \approx 0.84510

\log 5 = \log\!\left(\frac{10}{2}\right) = 1 - \log 2 \approx 0.69897

\log 4 = 2\log 2, \quad \log 6 = \log 2 + \log 3, \quad \log 8 = 3\log 2

\log 9 = 2\log 3, \quad \log 12 = 2\log 2 + \log 3

Expressing in terms of given logs:
If  \log_x 7 = p,\; \log_x 3 = q,\; \log_x 5 = r, then:
\log_x 4.2 = \log_x\!\left(\frac{42}{10}\right)
= \log_x(2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7) - \log_x(2 \cdot 5)
= \log_x 3 + \log_x 7 - \log_x 5
= q + p - r

Logarithm equations --- isolate, then exponentiate:
2\log k = 9 \rightarrow \log k = \frac{9}{2} \rightarrow k = 10^{4.5}

\log k = 20 \rightarrow k = 10^{20}
Remainder of  k \div 6: use  10^{20}  mod  6
10 \equiv 4 (mod  6 ),\; 10^2 \equiv 4 (mod  6 ),\; so  10^{20} \equiv 4 (mod  6 )

Exponential equations --- take log of both sides:
4^{6k+2} = 16^k \rightarrow 2^{2(6k+2)} = 2^{4k} \rightarrow 12k+4 = 4k \rightarrow k = -\frac{1}{2}

Natural log to solve:  10 = (20)(2^{2x})
\rightarrow 2^{2x} = \frac{1}{2} \rightarrow 2x = -1 \rightarrow x = -\frac{1}{2}

Equal bases: set exponents equal.
Otherwise: take log of both sides and apply the power rule.

Chain rule of logs:
\log_a(b) \cdot \log_b(c) = \log_a(c)
