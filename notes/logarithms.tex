1. Definition: $\log_b x = n$ if and only if $b^n = x$

2. Log rules

$$ \log_b(xy) = \log_b x + \log_b y $$

$$ \log_b \left( \frac{x}{y} \right) = \log_b x - \log_b y $$

$$ \log_b(x^n) = n \log_b x $$

Change of base:

$$ \log_b a = \frac{\log a}{\log b} $$

3. Key values

$$ \log 2 \approx 0.30103, \quad \log 3 \approx 0.47712, \quad \log 7 \approx 0.84510 $$

$$ \log 5 = 1 - \log 2 \approx 0.69897 $$

$$ \log 4 = 2\log 2, \quad \log 6 = \log 2 + \log 3, \quad \log 8 = 3\log 2 $$

4. If $\log_x 7 = p$, $\log_x 3 = q$, $\log_x 5 = r$, find $\log_x 4.2$:

$$ \log_x 4.2 = \log_x \left( \frac{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7}{2 \cdot 5} \right) = q + p - r $$

5. Solving log equations

$$ 2\log k = 9 \rightarrow k = 10^{4.5} $$

6. Remainder: log k = 20, so $k = 10^{20}$. Since 10 mod 6 = 4 and $4^n$ mod 6 = 4 for all n:

$$ 10^{20} \equiv 4 \quad (mod \; 6) $$

7. Exponential equation — convert to same base

$$ 4^{6k+2} = 16^k \rightarrow 2^{12k+4} = 2^{4k} \rightarrow 8k = -4 \rightarrow k = -\frac{1}{2} $$
