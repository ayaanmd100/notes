1. 2x2 determinant

$$ \det \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc $$

2. 2x2 inverse

$$ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix} $$

No inverse when det = 0.

3. Matrix multiplication

$$ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} e & f \\ g & h \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} ae+bg & af+bh \\ ce+dg & cf+dh \end{pmatrix} $$

4. Matrix equation: $AX = B$ means $X = A^{-1}B$

5. 3x3 determinant (cofactor expansion along row 1)

$$ \det \begin{pmatrix} a & b & c \\ d & e & f \\ g & h & i \end{pmatrix} = a(ei-fh) - b(di-fg) + c(dh-eg) $$

6. No-inverse example

$$ \det \begin{pmatrix} 24 & 16 \\ k & -8 \end{pmatrix} = 0 \rightarrow -192-16k=0 \rightarrow k=-12 $$

7. Identity matrix

$$ I = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} $$

8. Solving matrix equation: entry (1,2) of

$$ \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 4 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} -1 & k \\ -1 & 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} $$

gives $5k + 20 = 0$, so $k = -4$.
