1. Arithmetic mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$

2. Weighted mean

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{n_1 \bar{x}_1 + n_2 \bar{x}_2}{n_1+n_2} $$

3. Example: mean 32 over 50, mean 53 over 70

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{50(32)+70(53)}{120} = \frac{5310}{120} = \frac{177}{4} $$

4. Median: sort data, take middle value (or average of two middle values for even n).

Scores: 62, 68, 73, 77, 78, 82, 86, 86, 90, 92

$$ Median = \frac{78+82}{2} = 80 $$

5. Mode: most frequently occurring value.

6. Adjusted score: adding (1/10) of median to each score. Adjustment = 80/10 = 8. Lowest score 62 becomes 70.

$$ \frac{8}{62} \times 100 \approx 12.903\% \; increase $$

7. Harmonic mean

$$ H = \frac{2ab}{a+b} $$

Harmonic mean of 10 and 24:

$$ H = \frac{2(10)(24)}{34} = \frac{480}{34} = \frac{240}{17} $$

8. Average speed = total distance / total time. (1/3)d at 70 km/h, (2/3)d at 35 km/h:

$$ t = \frac{d/3}{70} + \frac{2d/3}{35} = \frac{5d}{210}, \quad \bar{v} = \frac{d}{5d/210} = 42 \; km/h $$

9. Mean of $S = \{1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5\}$

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{5}\right) = \frac{77}{240} $$
