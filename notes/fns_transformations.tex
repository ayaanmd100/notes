=====================================
FUNCTIONS AND TRANSFORMATIONS
=====================================
 
61. Transformation  g(x) = A f(B(x - h)) + k
Point (a, b) on f maps to:
$$ \left( \frac{a}{B} + h, \;\; Ab + k \right) \quad \text{on } g $$
Horizontal shift +h,  horizontal scale 1/B,  vertical scale A,  vertical shift +k.
 
62. Vertex of  y = ax^2 + bx + c
$$ x_v = -\frac{b}{2a} \qquad y_v = c - \frac{b^2}{4a} $$
 
63. Maximum or minimum of quadratic
$$ y_{\max/\min} = \frac{4ac - b^2}{4a} $$
Maximum when a < 0,   minimum when a > 0.
 
64. Inverse function
To find f^{-1}(x): swap x and y, solve for y.
$$ f\left(f^{-1}(x)\right) = x \qquad f^{-1}\!\left(f(x)\right) = x $$
 
65. Composition of functions
$$ (f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) $$
