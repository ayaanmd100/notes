=====================================
CONIC SECTIONS
=====================================
 
56. Circle  (center (h,k), radius r)
$$ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 $$
 
57. Completing the square
$$ x^2 + bx = \left( x + \frac{b}{2} \right)^2 - \frac{b^2}{4} $$
 
58. Ellipse  (center (h,k), a > b > 0)
$$ \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1 $$
Major axis = 2a,   minor axis = 2b.
 
59. Parabola opening up/down  (vertex (h,k), p = signed distance to focus)
$$ y - k = \frac{1}{4p}(x - h)^2 $$
Focus at (h, k+p),   directrix  y = k - p.
 
60. Parabola opening right/left  (vertex (h,k))
$$ x - h = \frac{1}{4p}(y - k)^2 $$
Focus at (h+p, k),   directrix  x = h - p.
