LOGARITHMS AND EXPONENTS
=====================================

7. Definition
$$ \log_b x = y \quad \text{iff} \quad b^y = x \qquad b > 0,\; b \ne 1,\; x > 0 $$

8. Core identities
$$ \log_b 1 = 0 \qquad \log_b b = 1 \qquad b^{\log_b x} = x $$

9. Product rule
$$ \log_b(MN) = \log_b M + \log_b N $$

10. Quotient rule
$$ \log_b \left( \frac{M}{N} \right) = \log_b M - \log_b N $$

11. Power rule
$$ \log_b \left( M^p \right) = p \log_b M $$

12. Change of base
$$ \log_b x = \frac{\log x}{\log b} = \frac{\ln x}{\ln b} $$

13. Natural log identities
$$ \ln e^x = x \qquad e^{\ln x} = x $$

14. Solving exponential equations
Equal bases: set exponents equal.
Otherwise: take log of both sides and apply the power rule.
Also, try to manipuklate usining exponent rules to simplify it.
