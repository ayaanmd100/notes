1. Equation-equals-1 case analysis: $(f(x))^{g(x)} = 1$

- Case 1: $g(x) = 0$ (base is nonzero)
- Case 2: $f(x) = 1$
- Case 3: $f(x) = -1$ and $g(x)$ is even

Example: $(x^2-5x+5)^{x^2-11x+30} = 1$

Case 1: x=5,6. Case 2: x=1,4. Case 3: x=2,3 (exponents 12 and 6, both even).

$$ Sum = 1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21 $$

2. Pigeonhole Principle

$$ \frac{k(k+1)}{2} \ge 200 \rightarrow k_{\max}=19 $$

(19 holes, distinct counts 1 through 19 sum to 190)

3. Greatest percentage increase for lowest score s with added amount m:

$$ \frac{m}{s} \times 100 $$

4. Space diagonal of rectangular box with edges a, b, c

$$ d = \sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2} $$

Given face diagonals $p, q, r$: $a^2+b^2+c^2 = \frac{p^2+q^2+r^2}{2}$

$$ d = \sqrt{\frac{p^2+q^2+r^2}{2}} $$

5. Sum of interior angles of n-gon: $(n-2) \cdot 180$

6. Exponent equation — take log of both sides when bases differ

$$ a^f = b^g \rightarrow f \log a = g \log b $$
