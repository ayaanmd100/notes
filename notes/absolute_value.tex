1. Definition

$$ |x| = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} x & x \ge 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{array} \right. $$

2. Equation $|f(x)| = k$: $f(x) = k$ or $f(x) = -k$.

3. Inequality $|f(x)| < k$ means $-k < f(x) < k$ (where $k > 0$).

4. Inequality $|f(x)| > k$ means $f(x) > k$ or $f(x) < -k$.

5. Example: $2|3x - 5| \le 20$

$$ |3x-5| \le 10 \rightarrow -\frac{5}{3} \le x \le 5 \rightarrow 7 \; integers $$

6. Example: $\left|\frac{2x+1}{3}\right| < 4$

$$ -\frac{13}{2} < x < \frac{11}{2} \rightarrow integers: -6 \; to \; 5, \; sum = -6 $$

7. Area bounded by $|x| + |y| = 20$ (diamond shape)

$$ A = \frac{1}{2}(40)(40) = 800 $$
