Binomial probability:
\Pr(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}

Expected value:  E(X) = np

Coin toss (2023 \#16): exactly 5 heads in 10 tosses,  p = \frac{1}{2}
\Pr(X=5) = \binom{10}{5}\!\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{10} = \frac{252}{1024} = \frac{63}{256}

True/false quiz (State 2024 \#15): 10 questions, score exactly 7,  p = \frac{1}{2}
\Pr(X=7) = \binom{10}{7}\!\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{10} = \frac{120}{1024} = \frac{15}{128}

Conditional probability:  \Pr(A | B) = \frac{\Pr(A \cap B)}{\Pr(B)}

Independent events:  \Pr(A \cap B) = \Pr(A) \cdot \Pr(B)

Mutually exclusive:  \Pr(A \cap B) = 0, so  \Pr(A \cup B) = \Pr(A) + \Pr(B)

Geometric distribution (first success on trial  k):
\Pr(X = k) = (1-p)^{k-1} p

Sum of geometric series in probability:
\Pr(win) = p + (1-p)^2 p + (1-p)^4 p + \cdot\cdot\cdot = \frac{p}{1-(1-p)^2}

Subsets of a set with  n  elements:  2^n  (including empty set)
S = \{A,L,G,T,W,O\},  |S| = 6 \rightarrow 2^6 = 64  subsets

Inclusion-exclusion:
\Pr(A \cup B) = \Pr(A) + \Pr(B) - \Pr(A \cap B)
