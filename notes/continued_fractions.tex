1. Evaluate from innermost level outward.

2. For repeating pattern, set expression equal to x and solve.

Example: $x = 2 + \frac{1}{3 + \frac{1}{x}}$

$$ x = \frac{7x+2}{3x+1} \rightarrow 3x^2-6x-2=0 \rightarrow x = 1 \pm \frac{\sqrt{15}}{3} $$

Taking positive root:

$$ x = \frac{3+\sqrt{15}}{3} $$
