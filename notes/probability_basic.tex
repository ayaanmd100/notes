\Pr(A) = \frac{favorable outcomes}{total outcomes}

0 \le \Pr(A) \le 1

\Pr(not  A) = 1 - \Pr(A)

\Pr(A \cup B) = \Pr(A) + \Pr(B) - \Pr(A \cap B)

\Pr(A \cap B) = \Pr(A) \cdot \Pr(B) \quad (if independent)

Venn diagram (2020 \#2): 52 seniors
|M \cup S| = |M| + |S| - |M \cap S| = 24 + 10 - 5 = 29
|neither| = 52 - 29 = 23

At least one success in  n  independent trials:
\Pr(at least 1) = 1 - (1-p)^n

Example (2013 \#9):  p = 0.73, need  \Pr(at least 1) < 0.99
1 - (0.27)^n < 0.99 \rightarrow (0.27)^n > 0.01
n\log(0.27) > \log(0.01) \rightarrow n < \frac{2}{0.5686} \approx 3.52
\rightarrow maximum whole number:  n = 3

Geometric probability (alternating turns):
\Pr(M) = \frac{p}{1 - (1-p)(1-q)}

Molly  (p = \frac{3}{5}), Connie (q = \frac{3}{4}), Molly goes first:
\Pr(M) = \frac{3/5}{1 - (2/5)(1/4)} = \frac{3/5}{9/10} = \frac{2}{3}
