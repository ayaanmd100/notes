1. Basic probability: $\Pr(A) = \frac{favorable}{total}$, where $0 \le \Pr(A) \le 1$

2. Complement: $\Pr(\overline{A}) = 1 - \Pr(A)$

3. Addition rule

$$ \Pr(A \cup B) = \Pr(A) + \Pr(B) - \Pr(A \cap B) $$

4. Venn diagram example: 52 students, 10 A in science, 24 A in math, 5 both

$$ |M \cup S| = 29, \quad neither = 52-29 = 23 $$

5. At least one success in n independent trials

$$ \Pr(at \; least \; 1) = 1-(1-p)^n $$

6. Example: p = 0.73, find max n such that Pr < 0.99

$$ (0.27)^n > 0.01 \rightarrow n < \frac{-2}{\log(0.27)} \approx 3.52 \rightarrow n_{\max} = 3 $$

7. Dart threshold: 28 hits out of 112, need > 35%

$$ \frac{28+x}{112+x} > 0.35 \rightarrow 0.65x > 11.2 \rightarrow x_{\min} = 18 $$

8. Primality check ($6 < x < 15$, is $x^2 - 29$ prime?):
x=6: 7 (prime). x=7: 20. x=8: 35. x=9: 52. x=10: 71 (prime). x=11: 92. x=12: 115. x=13: 140. x=14: 167 (prime).

$$ \Pr = \frac{3}{9} = \frac{1}{3} $$
