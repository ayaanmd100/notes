1. Basic step

$$ x^2 + bx = \left(x + \frac{b}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{b^2}{4} $$

2. Vertex form of quadratic: $ax^2 + bx + c = a\!\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 + c - \frac{b^2}{4a}$

$$ h = -\frac{b}{2a}, \quad k = c - \frac{b^2}{4a} $$

3. Example: $2x^2 - 8x + 3$

$$ 2(x^2-4x)+3 = 2\left[(x-2)^2-4\right]+3 = 2(x-2)^2 - 5 $$

Vertex: (2, -5).

4. Completing the square for circles

$$ x^2+2x+y^2-12y+3=0 \rightarrow (x+1)^2+(y-6)^2=34 $$

Center (-1, 6), radius $\sqrt{34}$.

5. General conic form

$$ Ax^2+Bx+Cy^2+Dy+E=0 \rightarrow A\!\left(x+\frac{B}{2A}\right)^2 + C\!\left(y+\frac{D}{2C}\right)^2 = \frac{B^2}{4A}+\frac{D^2}{4C}-E $$

6. If a > 0: minimum = k (vertex y-value). If a < 0: maximum = k.

Example: $f(x) = -2x^2 + 5x + 3$

$$ h = \frac{5}{4}, \quad k = 3 + \frac{25}{8} = \frac{49}{8} $$

7. Finding k such that min of $f(x) = x^2 - 2kx - k$ equals -6.

Vertex y-value: $-k - k^2 = -6$

$$ k^2+k-6=0 \rightarrow (k+3)(k-2)=0 $$

k = -3 or k = 2, sum = -1.
