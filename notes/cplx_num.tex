=====================================
COMPLEX NUMBERS
=====================================

1. Imaginary unit and cycle of powers
$$ i = \sqrt{-1} \qquad i^2 = -1 \qquad i^3 = -i \qquad i^4 = 1 $$

2. Reducing i^n  (divide exponent by 4, use remainder r)
$$ i^n = i^r \quad \text{where} \quad r = n \;\text{mod}\; 4 $$

3. Example reduction
$$ i^{2026} \to 2026 = 4 \times 506 + 2 \to i^{2026} = i^2 = -1 $$

4. Sum of consecutive powers  (any 4 consecutive powers sum to 0)
$$ i^k + i^{k+1} + i^{k+2} + i^{k+3} = 0 $$

5. Arithmetic with complex numbers
$$ (a + bi) + (c + di) = (a+c) + (b+d)i $$
$$ (a + bi)(c + di) = (ac - bd) + (ad + bc)i $$

6. Modulus
$$ \left| a + bi \right| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} $$
