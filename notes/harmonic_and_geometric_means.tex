1. Arithmetic: $A = \frac{a+b}{2}$. Geometric: $G = \sqrt{ab}$. Harmonic: $H = \frac{2ab}{a+b}$.

2. Ordering for positive numbers: $H \le G \le A$

3. AM-GM inequality (equality iff $a = b$)

$$ \frac{a+b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab} $$

4. For n numbers, harmonic mean

$$ H = \frac{n}{\frac{1}{x_1}+\frac{1}{x_2}+\cdot\cdot\cdot+\frac{1}{x_n}} $$
