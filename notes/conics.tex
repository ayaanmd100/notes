1. Circle: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$

2. Ellipse (horizontal major axis, $a > b > 0$)

$$ \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1, \quad c^2 = a^2-b^2 $$

Major axis = 2a, minor axis = 2b.

3. Hyperbola (horizontal transverse axis)

$$ \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1, \quad c^2 = a^2+b^2 $$

4. Parabola — vertical axis: $(x-h)^2 = 4p(y-k)$

Focus at $(h,\,k+p)$, directrix $y=k-p$.

5. Parabola — horizontal axis: $(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)$

Focus at $(h+p,\,k)$, directrix $x=h-p$.

6. Example: $x = \frac{1}{8}(y-3)^2$

$$ (y-3)^2 = 8(x-0) \rightarrow 4p=8 \rightarrow p=2 \rightarrow focus \; at \; (2,3) $$

7. Focus-directrix to equation: focus $(-2,3)$, directrix $y = -1$. Vertex $= (-2, 1)$, $p = 2$.

$$ (x+2)^2 = 8(y-1) \rightarrow x^2+4x-8y+12=0 $$

A+B+C+D+E = 1+0+4+(-8)+12 = 9.
