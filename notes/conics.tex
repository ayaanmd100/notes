--- Circle ---
(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2
Center  (h,k), radius  r

--- Ellipse (horizontal major axis) ---
\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1, \quad a > b > 0
Major axis length = 2a, \quad minor axis length = 2b
c^2 = a^2 - b^2, \quad foci at  (h \pm c,\, k)

--- Ellipse (vertical major axis) ---
\frac{(x-h)^2}{b^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{a^2} = 1, \quad a > b
Foci at  (h,\, k \pm c)

--- Hyperbola (horizontal) ---
\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} - \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1
c^2 = a^2 + b^2

--- Parabola (vertical axis) ---
(x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)
Vertex  (h,k), focus  (h,\, k+p), directrix  y = k - p

--- Parabola (horizontal axis) ---
(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)
Focus  (h+p,\, k), directrix  x = h - p

Example (2020 \#13):  x = \frac{1}{8}(y-3)^2
\rightarrow (y-3)^2 = 8(x-0)
\rightarrow 4p = 8 \rightarrow p = 2
\rightarrow focus at  (2,\, 3)
