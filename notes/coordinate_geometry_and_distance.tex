Distance between two points:
d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}

Midpoint:  M = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)

Slope:  m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}

Slope-intercept:  y = mx + b

Point-slope:  y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)

Perpendicular slopes:  m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1

Parallel: same slope, different intercept.

Distance from point  (x_0, y_0)  to line  Ax + By + C = 0:
d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}

Example (2020 \#12): distance from origin to  5x - 12y + 26 = 0
d = \frac{|0 + 0 + 26|}{\sqrt{25+144}} = \frac{26}{13} = 2

Distance between parallel lines  Ax+By+C_1=0  and  Ax+By+C_2=0:
d = \frac{|C_1 - C_2|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}

Line and circle intersection: substitute line into circle equation.
Discriminant tells number of intersections:
\Delta > 0: 2 points,\quad \Delta = 0: tangent,\quad \Delta < 0: none

Space diagonal with face diagonals  p, q, r:
a^2+b^2+c^2 = \frac{p^2+q^2+r^2}{2}
Space diagonal = \sqrt{\frac{p^2+q^2+r^2}{2}}

Space diagonal of box (dimensions  l, w, h):
d = \sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2}

Area of Triangle via Coordinates (determinant form):
Area = \frac{1}{2} \left| \det \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & y_1 & 1 \\ x_2 & y_2 & 1 \\ x_3 & y_3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \right|
