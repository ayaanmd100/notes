1. Distance: $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$

2. Midpoint: $M = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\; \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$

3. Slope: $m = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$. Perpendicular: $m_1 m_2 = -1$.

4. Point-to-line distance

$$ d = \frac{|Ax_0+By_0+C|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}} $$

5. Distance from origin to $5x - 12y + 26 = 0$

$$ d = \frac{26}{\sqrt{169}} = \frac{26}{13} = 2 $$

6. Distance between parallel lines

$$ d = \frac{|C_1-C_2|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}} $$

7. Line-circle intersection: $y=x+1$ and $x^2+y^2=25$

$$ x^2+(x+1)^2=25 \rightarrow x=-4, \; 3 $$

Points: (-4,-3) and (3,4). Distance:

$$ d = \sqrt{7^2+7^2} = 7\sqrt{2} $$

8. Space diagonal from face diagonals p, q, r

$$ space \; diagonal = \sqrt{\frac{p^2+q^2+r^2}{2}} $$
