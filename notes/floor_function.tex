1. Floor: greatest integer at or below x.

$$ \lfloor 3.7 \rfloor = 3, \quad \lfloor -2.79 \rfloor = -3 $$

Negatives floor toward negative infinity.

2. Ceiling: smallest integer at or above x.

$$ \lceil 3.2 \rceil = 4, \quad \lceil -2.1 \rceil = -2 $$

3. Example

$$ \lfloor 35.5 \rfloor + \lfloor -2.79 \rfloor - \lfloor 10.25 \rfloor = 35+(-3)-10 = 22 $$
