Direct:  y = kx \quad (y \propto x)

Inverse:  y = \frac{k}{x} \quad (xy = k)

Joint:  y = kxz \quad (y \propto xz)

Combined:  y = \frac{kxz}{w}

Find  k  from initial conditions, then solve for unknown.

Example (2022 \#4):  x  varies jointly with  y  and  z
x = 25  when  y = 6,\, z = 10 \rightarrow 25 = k(6)(10) \rightarrow k = \frac{5}{12}
x = \frac{5}{12}(20)(30) = 250
