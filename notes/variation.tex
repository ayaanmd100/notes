1. Direct: $y = kx$. Inverse: $y = k/x$. Joint: $y = kxz$.

2. Algorithm: write equation, solve for k, find unknown.

3. Example: x varies jointly with y and z, $x=25$ when $y=6$, $z=10$

$$ 25 = k(6)(10) \rightarrow k = \frac{5}{12} \rightarrow x = \frac{5}{12}(20)(30) = 250 $$

4. Inverse proportion: $x_1 y_1 = x_2 y_2$
