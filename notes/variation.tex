=====================================
9. APPLIED ALGEBRA AND VARIATION
=====================================

Direct variation:
$$y = kx$$

Inverse variation:
$$y = \frac{k}{x} \qquad \text{equivalently} \qquad xy = k$$

Joint variation (z varies jointly as x and y):
$$z = kxy$$

Combined variation example (x varies directly as z and w^3, inversely as y):
$$x = \frac{k \cdot z \cdot w^3}{y}$$

Work-rate formula:
$$\text{Rate} \times \text{Time} = \text{Work}$$
$$\text{rate per worker} = \frac{\text{work}}{\text{workers} \times \text{time}}$$

Optimizing product given fixed sum (AM-GM applied):
When x + y = c (constant), xy is maximized when x = y = c/2:
$$(xy)_{\max} = \frac{c^2}{4}$$

Compound Interest:
$$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Continuous Compounding:
$$A = P \exp(rt)$$
