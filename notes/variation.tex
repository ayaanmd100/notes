 
=====================================
VARIATION
=====================================
 
66. Direct variation
$$ y = kx $$
 
67. Inverse variation
$$ y = \frac{k}{x} \qquad \text{equivalently} \qquad xy = k $$
 
68. Joint variation  (z varies jointly as x and y)
$$ z = kxy $$
 
69. Combined variation example
x varies directly as z and w^3, inversely as y:
$$ x = \frac{k \cdot z \cdot w^3}{y} $$
 
70. Work-rate formula
$$ \text{Rate} \times \text{Time} = \text{Work} $$
$$ \text{rate per worker} = \frac{\text{work}}{\text{workers} \times \text{time}} $$
