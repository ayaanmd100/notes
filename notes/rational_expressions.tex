1. Adding fractions

$$ \frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad+bc}{bd} $$

2. Solving rational equations: multiply by LCD, check extraneous solutions.

3. Example: $\frac{5}{x} - \frac{3}{x+4} = 2$

$$ 5(x+4)-3x = 2x(x+4) \rightarrow x^2+3x-10=0 \rightarrow x=-5 \; or \; x=2 $$

4. Vertical asymptotes: where denominator = 0.

5. Horizontal asymptotes by comparing degrees:
- deg(num) < deg(den): $y = 0$
- deg(num) = deg(den): $y$ = ratio of leading coefficients
- deg(num) > deg(den): no horizontal asymptote
