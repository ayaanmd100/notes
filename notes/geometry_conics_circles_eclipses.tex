=====================================
7. DISTANCE, GEOMETRY, AND CONIC SECTIONS
=====================================

Distance between two points:
$$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$

Midpoint formula:
$$M = \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \;\; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)$$

Distance from point (x_0, y_0) to line ax + by + c = 0:
$$d = \frac{\left| ax_0 + by_0 + c \right|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$$

Distance between parallel lines ax + by + c_1 = 0 and ax + by + c_2 = 0:
$$d = \frac{\left| c_1 - c_2 \right|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$$

Space diagonal of rectangular box (dimensions l, w, h):
$$d = \sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2}$$

Area of Triangle via Coordinates:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \det \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & y_1 & 1 \\ x_2 & y_2 & 1 \\ x_3 & y_3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \right|$$

Radical product and quotient:
$$\sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{ab}$$
$$\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}$$

Rationalizing the denominator:
$$\frac{k}{\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}} = \frac{k\left(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}\right)}{a - b}$$

Circle (center (h,k), radius r):
$$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$$

Completing the square:
$$x^2 + bx = \left( x + \frac{b}{2} \right)^2 - \frac{b^2}{4}$$

Ellipse (center (h,k), a > b > 0):
$$\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2} + \frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2} = 1$$
$$\text{Major axis} = 2a \qquad \text{Minor axis} = 2b$$

Parabola opening up/down (vertex (h,k), p = signed distance to focus):
$$y - k = \frac{1}{4p}(x - h)^2$$
Focus at (h, k+p), directrix y = k - p.

Parabola opening right/left (vertex (h,k)):
$$x - h = \frac{1}{4p}(y - k)^2$$
Focus at (h+p, k), directrix x = h - p.
