1. Arrangements of n objects with repeated types

$$ \frac{n!}{r_1! \, r_2! \, r_3! \cdot\cdot\cdot} $$

2. CHOICE (C repeats twice)

$$ \frac{6!}{2!} = 360 $$

3. NATIONAL (N repeats twice, A repeats twice)

$$ \frac{8!}{2! \cdot 2!} = 10080 $$

4. Probability that 3 letters drawn from NATIONAL spell TAN

$$ \frac{\binom{1}{1} \binom{2}{1} \binom{2}{1}}{\binom{8}{3}} = \frac{4}{56} = \frac{1}{14} $$

5. Three-digit numbers, no zeroes, at least one 5

$$ 9^3 - 8^3 = 729 - 512 = 217 $$

6. Circular arrangements of n objects: $(n-1)!$
