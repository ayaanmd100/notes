Arrangements of  n  objects where  r_1  are identical of type 1,
r_2  identical of type 2, etc.:
\frac{n!}{r_1!\, r_2!\, r_3!\, \cdot\cdot\cdot}

CHOICE: 6 letters, C repeats twice:
\frac{6!}{2!} = \frac{720}{2} = 360

NATIONAL: 8 letters, N repeats twice, A repeats twice:
\frac{8!}{2!\,2!} = \frac{40320}{4} = 10080

Three letters from NATIONAL that spell TAN:
Bag: \{N,A,T,I,O,N,A,L\; T: 1 copy, A: 2 copies, N: 2 copies}
Ways to choose 1 T, 1 A, 1 N:  \binom{1}{1}\binom{2}{1}\binom{2}{1} = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 4
Total ways to draw 3 from 8:  \binom{8}{3} = 56
\Pr(TAN) = \frac{4}{56} = \frac{1}{14}

Circular arrangements:  (n-1)!

Three-digit numbers with no zeroes and at least one 5:
Total with no zeroes:  9^3 = 729
No zeroes AND no fives:  8^3 = 512
At least one five (no zeroes):  729 - 512 = 217

Number of integers in range  [a, b]:  \lfloor b \rfloor - \lceil a \rceil + 1
