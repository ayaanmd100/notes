=====================================
5. COMBINATORICS AND PROBABILITY
=====================================

Permutations (ordered, r chosen from n):
$$P(n,r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$$

Combinations (unordered, r chosen from n):
$$\binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$$

Arrangements with repeated elements:
$$\frac{n!}{n_1! \, n_2! \cdot \cdot \cdot n_k!}$$

Binomial Theorem:
$$(x + y)^n = \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k} y^k$$

General (k+1)-th term in binomial expansion:
$$T_{k+1} = \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k} y^k$$

Sum of all binomial coefficients (set x = y = 1):
$$\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} = 2^n$$

Complement rule:
$$\Pr(A^c) = 1 - \Pr(A)$$

Probability of at least one success in n independent trials:
$$\Pr(\text{at least one}) = 1 - (1 - p)^n$$

Inclusion-exclusion (two sets):
$$\left| A \cup B \right| = \left| A \right| + \left| B \right| - \left| A \cap B \right|$$

Binomial Probability (exactly k successes):
$$\Pr(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$$
