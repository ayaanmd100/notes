=====================================
COMBINATORICS AND PROBABILITY
=====================================

36. Permutations  (ordered, r chosen from n)
$$ P(n,r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} $$

37. Combinations  (unordered, r chosen from n)
$$ \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!} $$

38. Arrangements with repeated elements
$$ \frac{n!}{n_1!\, n_2!\, \cdots\, n_k!} $$

39. Binomial theorem
$$ (x + y)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k}\, y^k $$

40. General (k+1)-th term in binomial expansion
$$ T_{k+1} = \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k}\, y^k $$

41. Sum of all binomial coefficients  (set x = y = 1)
$$ \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} = 2^n $$

42. Complement rule
$$ \Pr(A^c) = 1 - \Pr(A) $$

43. Probability of at least one success in n independent trials
$$ \Pr(\text{at least one}) = 1 - (1 - p)^n $$

44. Inclusion-exclusion (two sets)
$$ \left| A \cup B \right| = \left| A \right| + \left| B \right| - \left| A \cap B \right| $$
