1. Revenue = price * quantity. Both are linear, so R is quadratic.

2. Maximum revenue at vertex: $p^* = -B/(2A)$ where $R = Ap^2 + Bp + C$.

3. Example: 360 dinners at $50, every $5 increase removes 20 customers. Solve for p giving $19600.

$$ R = (50+5t)(360-20t) = -100t^2+800t+18000 $$

$$ t = \frac{800}{200}=4 \rightarrow p = \$70 $$

4. Example: $10/ride, 300 riders, $1 increase removes 15 riders.

$$ R = (10+x)(300-15x) = -15x^2+150x+3000 \rightarrow x=5 \rightarrow p=\$15 $$
